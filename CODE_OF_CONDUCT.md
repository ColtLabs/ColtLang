# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and acted on in a way appropriate to the circumstances, up to removal from the project. The owner should provide a private contact route before opening contributions broadly.
